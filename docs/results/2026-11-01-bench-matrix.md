## Findings
1. **Bead count at the camera's rates (script, stage steps included).** Tracking median per frame: 50 beads numba
   1.4-2.7 ms, GPU 1.8-2.7; 100 beads numba 2.6-4.0 ms, GPU 2.3-4.2. Against the frame budget: 90 Hz (11.1 ms) and
   150 Hz (6.7 ms) 0 drops in all 24 runs; 248 Hz (4.0 ms) one run drops (100 beads, phase_spline numba: 206 of
   6200); 555 Hz (1.8 ms) 10 of 12 runs drop, up to 14 053 of 14 000 (100 beads): **the half-ROI maximum is usable to
   about 20 beads, 248 Hz to about 100 on numba**, and the GPU gives no headroom here (its 2 ms floor).
2. **Accuracy does not depend on the bead count**: reference-corrected focus-step error median 1-12 nm at 50 / 100
   beads at every rate (one outlier: 90 Hz, 100 beads, phase_spline GPU, bead 1 jumped - median 1158 nm). Noise
   grows with the rate as before (10-33 nm at 90 Hz, 24-49 at 150, 16-65 at 248, 20-93 at 555).
3. **The stage xy steps**: 1 and 2 um steps keep the beads; the 5 um step (60 px) loses all four for the hold and
   they are re-acquired at 0 (lost 1000-4000 frames per run, stage raw up to 2.5 um = the lost-bead values).
   After every run with stage steps the beads sat 0.1-0.2 um further in +x: the ASI stage does not come back to the
   same place (read-back says it does; the sensor says 1 um per 12 runs), see 6.
4. **Focus drive speed has no effect on the measured accuracy.** Overshoot right after arrival: median 14-63 nm at
   4.958 mm/s, 11-54 nm at 0.4958 mm/s, method by method the same within scatter; reference-corrected step error
   1-7 nm at both speeds for 20, 50 and 100 nm steps and the +-1 um pattern. The 20 nm staircase is resolved: raw
   step error 28-77 nm (drift, as before), corrected 1-4 nm median. The 1 um/s condition (0.001 mm/s) was not
   reached (block refused by the drift at 23:20).
5. **The GUI client at 90 Hz is the script**: 30 runs, 0 gaps, 0 drops, 5-100 beads; tracking medians equal to the
   script's; the GUI tick 5-30 ms median (its own drawing, left alone on the owner's word). At 150 Hz the 5-bead
   runs are clean (0 drops, corrected error 1-4 nm); the later runs are drift, not GUI.
6. **The sample drifted all evening and ran away at the end.** Bead 1 in x: 1088 px (20:06) -> 1097 (21:07, 9 px/h
   with focus + magnet steps only) -> 1122 (21:31) -> 1147 (21:52) -> 1171 (22:24) -> 1198 (22:52) -> not found at
   23:19 (about 40 px/h once the stage-step blocks ran, in jumps of 6-25 px per block), and the focus left the ring
   pattern between 22:52 and 23:19. Every block re-finds the beads first (Croquette iterated from the last found
   positions, `bead_picks_latest.json`, all four must shift alike; since 23:20 a 90-px grid search of the common
   shift), which carried the matrix for two hours; a focus change it cannot recover.
7. **Tooling**: the rig returned to its initial condition after every block (camera rate / ROI / exposure, focus
   speed, lock); the runner stops when a lock stays held or a STOP file appears. One block of the first attempt
   (21:18) failed at the pick because the beads had moved 19 px during the previous block; fixed by starting from the
   last found positions. `JaiCamera` now reads the rate cap from the driver at the ROI in force; `api.focus.speed /
   set_speed` added (ASI `S Z`, capped at the opening speed, restored at close).
