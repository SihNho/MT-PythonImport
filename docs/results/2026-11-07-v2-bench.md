# spline_v2 / phase_spline_v2: the offline gates, and the rig bench that could not start (2026-11-07 09:30-10:05)

Decision [0031](../decisions/0031-spline-v2-phase-spline-v2-and-the-fair-comparison.md). Owner (morning, after

the bench."

## What v2 changes (from the 11-07 data)
- `spline_v2`: the SSD replaced by a normalised correlation (scale-free; the SSD's minimum moves with an intensity
  difference between the live profile and the LUT: `spline_v1` sat 7 nm from the reference on the LabVIEW fixture),
  the golden section ends at a 1e-3 slice bracket instead of 30 fixed steps; the GPU sums are float64 (FP32 placed
  the flat maximum 1.1 nm off).
- `phase_spline_v2`: the angle of the amplitude-weighted sum instead of the weighted mean of per-element angles (no
  per-element wrap at low signal, no per-element atan2: `phase_spline_v1` was the slowest method on numba and as
  noisy as `current_v1` at 555 Hz), the bracket widens to +-2 slices before the quadratic fallback, bisection to
  1e-3 slice.
