## Findings
- **Drops (the zero-drop boundary).** The FP32 GPU dropped no frame in any run: 200 beads at 248 Hz (1.8-2.2 ms p50,
  3.2-3.5 p99) and 100 beads at 555 Hz in the ROI (1.1-1.2 ms p50, 2.0-2.1 p99: above the 1.8 ms period at p99, the
  queue absorbs it). numba FP64 holds 100 beads at 150 Hz (200: phase_spline_v1 924 drops), 100 at 248 Hz (200: 2.5-5.2 k
  drops, all methods) and 20 at 555 Hz (50: phase_spline_v1 4016; 100: 9-14 k). One camera stall: s555's 50-bead
  spline_v1 numba run, 61 gaps and a 116 ms frame.
- **Tracking time.** numba 0.30-0.49 ms per frame at 3-6 beads, 1.4-2.1 at 50, 2.6-3.9 at 100, 5-12 at 200 (phase_spline_v1
  the slowest). GPU FP32 0.96-1.56 ms at 3-100 beads (the half ROI 0.96-1.2, full frame 1.2-1.6) and 1.8-2.2 at 200; the
  FP64 kernel (m90, 20 beads) 1.84-2.02 ms against FP32's 1.48-1.53 with the same accuracy columns.
- **Focus step accuracy (reference-corrected, the 4-bead blocks).** Median over runs of the median |error|: current_v1
  2 nm (90 / 150 Hz), 5 nm (248 / 555 Hz; max 14-52); phase_spline_v1 2-7 nm (max 10-72); spline_v1 3 nm at 90 Hz rising to
  7 / 13 / 19 nm at 150 / 248 / 555 Hz (max 169). The raw (uncorrected) errors are 20-130 nm median: the focus drive's
  settle and the drift; the reference bead removes them, so the program's tethered-minus-reference extension is right
  at the few-nm level with current_v1 and phase_spline_v1 at every rate.
- **Noise and lost frames.** The per-hold z noise is 16-80 nm at 90-150 Hz for every method; at 555 Hz current_v1 and
  phase_spline_v1 are 58-137 nm, spline_v1 21-58. spline_v1 pays for it in lost bead-frames: 2-6 x the others (median
  per run 8080 against 1390 at 555 Hz, 1907 against 644 at 90 Hz). The floor is the +-1 um steps, which push every bead to
  the LUT's edge for 100-700 frames per bead per run.
- **Magnet.** The four beads left after 04:15 move at most 50 nm (reference-corrected) over 30 / 10 / 0 mm. The owner's bead
  3 moved -0.72..-0.86 um at 10 mm and -0.47..-0.71 um at 0 mm in every six-bead run from m90 (02:40) on, reproducibly:
  it was a tether-like bead all night before it came off at 04:00, and it is the magnet max column of m90 / s90 / s150 /
  s248 (noted in the HTML tables). Bead 5 was lost in 37 % of its frames from s248's fourth run.
- **Stage.** The 1 and 2 um steps: 10-35 nm reference-corrected median. The max columns (0.4-2.3 um) come from the 5 um
  step and its return, whose hold window still contains the move (per-bead std ~1 um, several hundred lost bead-frames):
  the tool's window, not the tracking.
- **The LUT frame count** (n150 / n248 / n555, 17 / 28 / 62 frames per slice): the step errors (medians 2-13 nm) and the noise
  are within the run-to-run spread of the 10-frame blocks at the same rate; no gain visible at 3-4 beads.
- **The focus at 1 um/s** (p20 / p50 / p100: 20 / 50 / 100 nm staircases): the steps resolve (median |error| 1-8 nm at 20 nm
  steps, 2-5 at 100 nm), the follow time 4-9 ms median (40-70 ms at the opening speed), overshoot 11-62 nm. ppm1 (+-1 um
  at 1 um/s): follow 1-10 ms, overshoot 11-41 nm; the per-hold noise of current_v1 / phase_spline_v1 is 135-148 nm there
  against spline_v1's 24-28.
- **The drift hold** moved 129 times over the 147 script runs (63 stage, 66 focus): the stage -0.5..-0.9 um in x and
  -0.2..-0.6 in y about every third run (the drift of 11-01's direction), the focus 0.1-0.3 um; the beads stayed within
  3 px of their LUT positions for 3.5 hours, until the beads themselves came loose. The stage went from (0.0239, -2.9383)
  to (0.0288, -2.9402) mm and the focus from 5.8 to 6.7-7.1 um over the night.
- **The GUI client** (g90 / g150 / g248 / g555, 05:41-06:28, 48 runs, the engine in its own process): the same picture as the
  script - 0 drops in 47 runs, the one exception the script also had (555 Hz, 50 beads, phase_spline_v1 numba: 3937
  drops, latency p99 118 ms); the GPU 0.97-1.58 ms p50 / 1.4-2.3 p99 at every rate, numba 0.28-0.47 at 3-4 beads and
  1.4-2.1 at 50. The window's own tick is 3-5 ms p50 / 9-16 max at 3-4 beads and 40-56 ms p50 / 135-213 max at 50 beads
  (the client's drawing of 50 beads, not the engine: the frames and drops are the engine's counts).
- **Conclusion.** The FP32 GPU with `current_v1` is the production default by the measured criteria: no drops at any rate
  or bead count tried, the same few-nm reference-corrected step error as numba FP64, 1-2 ms per frame flat in the bead
  count. `phase_spline_v1` equals it in accuracy and drops (on numba it is 30 % slower). `spline_v1` is the quietest per
  frame but loses 2-6 x the bead-frames and its step error grows with the rate, so it is not a default. The owner's
  acceptance numbers (decision 0030) are still the owner's to set; `current_v1` stays an option whatever they are.
